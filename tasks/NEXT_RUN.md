# Wakefall-first 250-sound run

The last expansion drew **237 Yes votes and 12 No votes** across 249 candidates. All 50 vehicle cues, all 15 new sci-fi device cues, and the full farm tools, animal care, animal voices, and habitat collections passed that pass. Seven of 11 watering cues passed. The rejected set concentrated in short physical interactions plus three fantasy spells. The votes identify sounds to revise. Individual causes are inferred from the recipes and the approved neighbors.

## Author and review

1. Finish roughly a dozen related sounds at a time inside each collection. Start with alternate wet crop actions and production stations for Wakefall.
2. Use the relevant approved reference to set perspective and level. For quiet gestures, start with isolated real CC0 or public-domain recordings. Let recorded surfaces and liquid do most of the work. Compare nearby cues for material, timing, and distance before adding variants.
3. Use different envelope shapes for fictional events that share effects, such as a portal opening, a short teleport, and an ice strike. Use Neon through Antigravity for design critique and public reference work.
4. Run QA with four seeds and both parameter extremes. Check closely related cues in short blind listening groups and send completed sounds through Quick Review. Keep the older vote and advance the revision when a sound returns.

See the [1,000-sound roadmap](ROADMAP.md) for overall library targets. This run is a prioritized slice across those families.
