# Eight synthesis patterns

Start with the event a player needs to recognize. Each blueprint below is a working TypeScript recipe. The linked recording modules contain the credited PCM takes and decode locally through `renderRecording`. Use the [source inventory](../sources/recordings.json) to find materials by title or source file. Keep `recording` and `additionalRecordings` on derivatives, including short excerpts.

1. **Firearm report.** Start with a short recorded attack chosen by seed. Underlay a dry pressure transient and give the environment its own quieter reflection. Leave a gap before a casing or action layer so the attack remains intelligible at low volume. Use `punch` for attack prominence and `space` when the player moves indoors. [Pistol blueprint](../recipes/pistol-9mm.ts)
2. **Explosion and debris.** Separate the initial crack, irregular low rumble, and material that falls afterward. Choose one foreground material such as stone or glass. Place its first contact after the pressure front and vary the gaps and gains of later pieces. Check the sub-band measurement and try the balanced mix with nearby UI sounds. [Grenade and rubble](../recipes/grenade-explosion.ts) · [Glass shower](../recipes/explosion-through-plate-glass-shower.ts)
3. **Engine and RPM.** Pair idle and loaded recordings with different spectra. Blend them with normalized load and keep the shafts' pitch motion continuous across changes. Leave shifts and contact noises to one-shot triggers. Check the loop boundary at both ends of the RPM parameter. [GT engine blueprint](../recipes/v10-inspired-gt-pit-lane-idle-loop.ts)
4. **Weather loop.** Build a continuous bed, a separate surface response, and sparse individual drops or gusts. Render through the continuation frames supplied by the engine. Keep the bed active at the seam. For a continuously adjustable storm, crossfade two running recipes with `SfxBedBlend`. [Rain blueprint](../recipes/light-rain-loop.ts)
5. **Water and splash.** Put the first contact at the event time. Follow with the broader water displacement, then irregular droplets or a retreating wash. A soft low-passed wash behind a clear near contact conveys mass on small speakers. Check that increasing size leaves the near contact audible. [Cove arrival splash](../recipes/canvas-kayak-in-a-cove-arrival-splash.ts)
6. **Footsteps.** Choose several distinct recorded contacts on the intended surface. Stage weight transfer, sole contact and release according to gait. Give grass a low mix level and sparse blades. Let gravel produce separate little stones. Alternate seeds for consecutive steps and check at least four in sequence. [Grass contact](../recipes/grass-footstep.ts) · [Gravel contact](../recipes/gravel-footstep.ts)
7. **Creature voice.** Preserve the source call's inflection, breath and pauses. Adjust phrase spacing independently from pitch. Use formant filtering sparingly when a fictional animal needs a different body size. Test for flat, persistent harmonic buzz and listen for identical repeated syllables. [Robin phrase](../recipes/robin-song.ts)
8. **Tactile UI.** Give hover, engagement, and release different gestures. For confirmation, sequence a light touch followed by a decisive detent. Keep the tail short enough for rapid navigation. For a warning, change the timing pattern so it remains identifiable at the same mix level. [Focus microswitch](../recipes/menu-option-focus-microswitch.ts) · [Elimination confirmation](../recipes/score-relay-elimination-confirm.ts)

For a new recipe, return exactly `ctx.frames` samples, derive timing from `ctx.sampleRate`, and use the supplied seed or `ctx.random()` for variation. Use `layer`, `mix`, `biquad`, `punch` and `modal` from the [DSP runtime](../engine/dsp.ts). A custom one-shot can set `immediateOnset: true` to trim to its attack and test onset in the first millisecond. Run the file-based QA command from the [skill](../SKILL.md) before wiring the event.
