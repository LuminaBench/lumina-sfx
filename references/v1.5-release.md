# Lumina SFX v1.5.0 release candidate

An agent can choose who is listening, which sound carries a scene, and where the other sounds sit. Every catalog recipe can be used as an independent spatial emitter. The local RC9 has 2,863 recipes.

## RC9 local editorial trials

Recurring inputs share their physical device and sound family. The scorer checks original transients around marked inputs, supports approved project profiles, and carries compatible approvals into a recut. The scene monitor measures the delivered phone-band and mono folds including focus transitions. Two stationary sources cover a tractor engine and helicopter rotor. Each uses a texture derived from its credited recording and leaves the trajectory to the game or picture.

The 100 existing selection briefs passed. The independent first edit trial initially matched 5 of 12 complete briefs. A blind Gemini 4 Neon listener rated 5 of those 12 original mixes ready, 1 in need of a minor edit and 6 in need of a major edit. Its judgments used anonymous audio and a written picture description. The repaired scorer subsequently met all 12 original picture rubrics and preserved all 17 silent regions in the effects stems.

A second set of twelve unseen cuts matched six complete picture rubrics on the first attempt. The other six exposed a whole word withheld during speech, an unnecessary touch Back cue, a physical Undo scored as interface feedback and three world-source selection or duration misses. The second set preserved thirteen of fourteen silent regions and kept the source video bitstream on all nine playable scores. These remaining cases guide the next candidate.

Two controlled listening scenes also ran across four seeds. At the reference seed, a directed shelter mix put the fire 5.93 dB above its combined backgrounds at the tenth percentile on a mono phone proxy. The unadjusted scene measured −19.95 dB. With a locked roof, the mixer reached a feasible target and retained all authored gains when the roof made the target unreachable. The phone check uses a filtered waveform. It does not measure a particular phone speaker.

## RC7 picture scoring

Give the agent a recorded cut or frame-marked edit. It can now inspect a local video, plan the visible actions, reuse original contacts, place effects at the picture frame, export a stereo SFX stem, and compare two scored videos. A revision can change a single cue or trim every tab without reselecting the rest. The normal demo palette has 16 clean contacts and transitions. A named on-screen object or an explicit stylistic request can call for a different source. [Score a finished edit](launch-audio.md#score-a-finished-edit)

The 10-second Generate and Settings edit has three new contacts. Text streaming in and the final logo fade stay quiet. In the 8.5-second palette edit, eight key-downs follow the letters of *Tidepool*. The original Preview click stays in the soundtrack while the SFX stem carries the pickup, snap and panel sweep.

Six independent browser-captured edits exercised 30 observations, including a narrated walkthrough, a silent recording, a launch teaser and a controller menu. The first pass missed two active controller focus changes. All six subsequently passed the original checks. Both narrated contacts measured 6 dB below their unattenuated alternatives, and the original narration and tile contact stayed within 0.03 dB of their source-only windows in the encoded previews. All 16 default sounds were also rendered against a matching picture moment and tested in a mono phone band. These checks cover timing, levels and source fit. ## What to try

- Fly through a shower. The dry engine stays steady, rain hits the glazing and a distant thunderclap arrives from the left after two seconds. All three share one listener.
- For a directed edit, put a jet in a storm. Switch focus from engine to weather, hold both in focus, or let thunder cover the engine once. The mixer measures the combined backgrounds after distance, filtering and placement. Each authored section gets a steady gain and an eased handoff. Locks preserve authored levels.
- Orbit a source, turn the listener, or send a sound overhead. A headphone WAV uses measured human head responses. Live Web Audio uses the browser's HRTF. Speaker and mono outputs remain available. Recorded UI can stay fixed as the world turns.
- Ask for quieter wind. Stable layer labels preserve the fire's take, event timing and position. Review the dry stems, positioned solos, delivered margins and weakest overlap time before wiring a scene into the game.
- Open and close a hangar door on a running rain loop. The game can supply obstruction; opening the door restores brightness and changes the reflected sound.

## RC6 listening and delivery checks

- All 129 machine leads were reviewed on two separately seeded, anonymous excerpts with Gemini 4 Neon. Of these, 73 retained their masters, eight passed after repairs, eight are usable when their field ambience is explicitly requested, and 40 are held from automatic selection. The two audio hashes and listening observations are in [the flag audit](../qa/flag-auditions.json). A changed master or newly detected sound reopens its review.
- The installed selector handled 100 written briefs covering 90 sounds, five deliberate silences and five requests needing more detail. Across successive blinded first-pick auditions, the listener accepted 85, 86, 87 and finally 88 of the 90 sound excerpts. All five intentional silences passed the last audition. Requests for clarification are scored on the decision alone. These are regression briefs used during tuning. They do not measure accuracy on unseen prompts.
- The two remaining misses were a distant jet flyby and rain pattering on a loose garage door. A follow-up played the replacement choices at normal game levels. The listener accepted the recorded, spatialized flyby and the receding roof recording. The earlier garage loop remains available by explicit ID and is held from automatic and clean selection. The garage brief accepts a short receding performance because it requests patter without specifying a loop. [Results for each brief](../qa/selection-listening.tsv)
- Four sources ran for 75 seconds apiece. The short-loop waveform correlations were below 0.02 in magnitude for rain, fire, cabin turbine and motorboat. All 60 repeated UI actions had one separated event. The fire still has a fairly active crackle. In context the dry cockpit read as an aircraft cabin. In a separate unlabeled motor comparison, the listener heard a smaller engine. Aircraft identity remains less certain in isolation.
- All 2,859 default masters were distinct. All passed four seeds at default, minimum and maximum parameters in the original and balanced profiles. The revised masters were rechecked after the catalog sweep. The left/right, near/far and mono checks passed across all 2,859. The released-bank fingerprint check accounted for all 2,853 earlier IDs. Two classifier runs found no speech, input or wildlife flags among the 24 selected changed or newly added regression masters. These checks cannot prove that every recording is free of incidental sounds.
- Headless Chrome on this Mac passed nine combinations of output mode and source count, with zero browser errors or audio gaps. At 32 simultaneous headphone sources the 95th-percentile frame interval measured 16.7 ms. A locally installed orbit pack rendered 308,700 stereo frames and crossed the listener in both renders.
- Two 10-second weather scenes rendered the same recorded sources from the pilot and apron perspectives. A blind, level-matched omission test favored the authored cockpit scene. Four near-identical pairs were auditioned and grouped among alternatives. Exact IDs and opposite-direction gestures remain individually addressable.

Run the regression briefs with `npm run qa:selection -- /tmp/selection.json`. Create anonymous stereo reels with `node --experimental-strip-types qa/render-selection-reels.mjs /tmp/selection.json /tmp/selection-reels` using a new destination. Add `--balanced` for normal game levels. For a blind review, give the listener only the WAVs and prompt sheets. Keep the answer key separate. [Written briefs](../qa/selection-briefs.tsv)

The 2,856 default masters each have a distinct PCM fingerprint. Every recipe passed four release seeds at default, minimum and maximum settings in both mastering profiles (34,272 seed/setting combinations). The packaged source manifest covers the original and derived PCM used by the studio.

The RC3 screen of two independent seeds across the whole catalog produced 26,966 classifier windows. The screen looks for speech, computer input, birds and insects. That screen produced the 129 leads re-auditioned in RC6. In RC3, clean selection admitted 478 of 481 source-isolated recipes. A missing label alone does not establish isolation. Bare requests for rain, thunder, a cockpit, or a fire resolve to one eligible source. The review flags are in `qa/source-holds.json`.

The lodge, airport and swimming scenes use continuous source performances. In the lodge rain stem, waveform correlation at the old 8.6-second period fell from 0.985 to −0.002. For the airport roof, the 6.43-second result fell from 0.995 to 0.008. The default flame uses longer open-damper wood-fire recordings. The swimmer enters and surfaces with two different recorded gestures while one motor continues along its path. All 41 revised cues passed four seeds at default, minimum and maximum controls and both output levels. Both screening seeds returned no background flags for those cues. A level-matched, blind Gemini 4 Neon comparison preferred the revised water and lodge excerpts. A follow-up reduced the close splash, extended the recorded drainage and added early room reflections around the hearth.
